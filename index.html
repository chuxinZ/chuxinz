About Me

Hey there, I'm Beibei! I grew up in Hebei Province, China, then headed to Shenyang, Liaoning for university. My master's journey took me to Uppsala, Sweden, and now I'm pursuing a Ph.D. at Université catholique de Louvain (UCLouvain) in Belgium.

Different cultures have both challenged and enriched me, especially when it comes to my love for food. I adore "驴肉火烧" (Donkey burgers) from my hometown, "东北一锅出" (One Pot Dish) which I fell in love with during my time in Northeast China, and indulge in the crispy delight of "锅包肉" (Guo Bao Rou). Sweden introduced me to the irresistible "kanelbulle" (cinnamon rolls) and tasty "köttbullar" (Swedish meatballs). And now, I've developed a fondness for "Liège waffles" (pearl sugar waffles).
